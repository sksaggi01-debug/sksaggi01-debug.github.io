Bureau of Economic Analysis · Analyst Training, Module 1

You are auditing the books of Cedar Flats, Texas.

Every transaction in town lands on your desk. Some belong in Gross Domestic Product. Most of the ones that look like they belong do not. Your job is to decide which is which, add up what survives, and then follow a single dollar all the way around the economy.

Population 41,200 · Reporting year 2026 · Four rounds, roughly 35 minutes
GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced in a country in a given time period.
Test 1Market value

Valued at the price it sold for. No price, no entry.

Test 2Final goods & services

Bought by its final user, and something was actually produced.

Test 3Produced within the country

Where it was made, not who owns the firm.

Test 4In a given time period

Produced this year. Not resold this year.

A transaction has to pass all four to enter GDP. Every wrong answer in Round 1 comes from one of these four tests.
