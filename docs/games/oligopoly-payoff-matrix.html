Price War · Prisoner's Dilemma
Both firms are tempted to cut prices to steal market share. The Nash Equilibrium (Low, Low) yields only 3, 3 — but mutual cooperation at (High, High) would yield 4, 4. This is the classic Prisoner's Dilemma: individual rationality leads to a collectively worse outcome.
