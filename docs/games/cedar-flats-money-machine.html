A town that runs on your decisions

Cedar Flats has $0 of GDP and no idea how to make any.

You run the money. Push a dollar into the town and watch where it goes — along the arrows, into somebody’s pocket, out to Vietnam, or nowhere at all. Nothing here is graded. Some moves make the GDP counter jump; some make it sit perfectly still, and those are the interesting ones.

Fourteen discoveries hidden in the machine · Built for ECON‑2301 by Dr. Sarabjeet Bedi
PHASE 1

The Money Machine

Free play. Click any transaction in town and watch the money travel. No turns, no limit, no wrong moves. Find all fourteen discoveries if you can.

PHASE 2

A Year in Cedar Flats

Twelve months. Each month the town faces a real decision and you choose. Then something happens that you did not choose. Your GDP at the end is whatever your year actually produced.

PHASE 3

Keep the Loop Running

Money leaks out of the economy through saving, taxes and imports. Put it back through investment, government and exports — or watch incomes shrink.

Three things before you start

  • Nothing is scored and nothing is timed. You cannot lose.
  • The counter only moves when something is produced. Money changing hands is not the same thing, and the machine will show you the difference.
  • A discovery unlocks the first time you make the machine behave unexpectedly. Chasing the strange results is the fastest way through.
Nothing is saved or sent. Refresh to reset the town.

Worth arguing about

  1. Your GDP number went up when the hurricane hit and the town rebuilt. Was Cedar Flats better off that year?
  2. Two towns produce the same things. In one, meals and childcare are bought in the market; in the other, they are done at home. Which one reports the higher GDP, and does it deserve to?
  3. You watched imports get added in C and taken back out in M. Why bother adding them at all instead of just ignoring them?
  4. In Phase 3, income only held steady when injections matched leakages. What does that tell you about what a recession actually is?
  5. Which single move in this whole game produced the most GDP, and which one felt the most useful to the people of Cedar Flats? Were they the same move?
