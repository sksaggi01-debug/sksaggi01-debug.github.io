How to play ▾
  1. Both players type a name. One clicks Host, the other clicks Join and enters the code.
  2. Each round shows the same real-world event. Make two predictions: (1) which curve shifts and which way, and (2) what happens to equilibrium price and quantity.
  3. Lock in your prediction. The graph reveals the shift and the correct answer, with the determinant explained.
  4. Score points for each correct call. Nail both in a round to build a streak for bonus points.
  5. Highest score after 7 events wins the extra credit.

On Zoom: two players in a breakout room. In person: two students at two laptops. Either way, same connection.
